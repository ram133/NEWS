# Advertisement Guidelines

NEWS stays free for readers by including a small number of product advertisements.

## Rules

- Maximum of 1–2 ads per issue
- Ads must be clearly labeled
- Only products relevant to AI, developers, productivity, or creators
- We reserve the right to reject any advertisement
- No misleading claims

## Ideal advertisers

- AI tools and platforms
- Developer infrastructure
- Productivity software
- Education / training products related to AI

## Contact

Open an issue in this repository with the subject "Advertising inquiry" or reach out via the contact methods listed in the main profile.
