## Top Stories

### 1. OpenAI: ChatGPT search volume reaches ~20% of Google’s
OpenAI reported that search-like queries through ChatGPT now equal about one-fifth of Google’s search volume. Weekly active users hit 1.2 billion (up from 1 billion roughly 60 days earlier).

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest public signal yet that ChatGPT is becoming a meaningful search competitor, not just a chat product.

Sources: OpenAI announcements / industry reports

---

### 2. AMD posts record quarter driven by AI data-center demand
AMD reported Q3 revenue of $13.4 billion (+45% YoY). Data-center revenue nearly doubled to $8.5 billion. The company guided Q4 higher and highlighted strong adoption of its Instinct GPUs and EPYC processors by major AI labs and cloud providers.

**Why it matters:** Confirms that the AI infrastructure buildout is still expanding rapidly beyond Nvidia.

Sources: AMD earnings

---

### 3. Anthropic open-sources FlashProve
Anthropic released FlashProve, a system that cryptographically proves an AI response came from a specific model (preventing silent model swaps by routers or intermediaries). The company also extended text watermarking and shipped Message Threads (≈35% faster time-to-first-token on long conversations).

**Why it matters:** Model identity and provenance are becoming real product and security requirements as AI systems are embedded deeper into workflows.

Sources: Anthropic / TechCrunch

---

### 4. Google bundles Search with AI tools for schools
Google will turn AI features (Gemini, NotebookLM, Classroom AI) off by default for K-12 accounts. Schools that keep AI disabled will also lose access to Google Search — the features are packaged together.

**Why it matters:** Forces a clear policy choice on schools and highlights how tightly Google is integrating AI into its core products.

Sources: Google / reporting
